North Pole OS

A festive circuit board brought to life with tilt-responsive parallax and a spinning cooling fan. Designed for portrait Android screens.

APK upload pending

The download will become available when the package is uploaded.

Installation instructions
  1. Download the APK on your Android device.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If prompted, allow installation from this source only if you trust the file.
  3. Install North Pole OS, then open it or select it from your device’s live wallpaper picker.
  4. Preview the wallpaper and select Set wallpaper. Choose the home screen or available screen options.
  5. Turn off the install-unknown-apps permission afterward if you enabled it.

Android only. Menu names and live wallpaper support vary by device. Not an iOS or Windows installer.

Christmas Clockwork

A snow globe frames a steam train and a lantern-lit Victorian village in this Christmas wallpaper made for Jen. Brass gears turn beneath the globe, and the scene shifts as you tilt your phone.

Download Android APK ↓

Direct download · Android installer

Installation instructions
  1. On your Android phone, tap Download Android APK, then open Christmas-Clockwork-for-Jen.apk from Downloads.
  2. If Android asks for permission, allow your browser or file manager to install this app. Tap Install. You can turn the permission off again after installation.
  3. Tap Open to launch the wallpaper preview. You can also open the Christmas Clockwork app or choose it from your live wallpaper picker.
  4. Tilt your phone to preview the movement. Tap Set wallpaper and choose from the screen options your phone offers.
